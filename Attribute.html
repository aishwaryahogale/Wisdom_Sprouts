Chatrapati Shivaji Maharaj

+

Chatrapati Shivaji Maharaj was the founder of the Maratha Empire .

"प्रतिपच्चंद्रलेखेव वर्धिष्णुर्विश्ववंदिता
शाहसूनोः शिवस्यैषा मुद्रा भद्राय राजते !"

Shivaji I was an Indian ruler and a member of the Bhonsle dynasty. Shivaji inherited a fiefdom from his father who served as a retainer for the Sultanate of Bijapur, which later formed the genesis of the Maratha Kingdom. In 1674, he was formally crowned the Chhatrapati of his realm at Raigad Fort.

Major Achievements & Legacy

Establishment of the Maratha Empire:
Shivaji Maharaj laid the foundation of the Maratha Empire, which became a significant power in India.
Fortifications and Naval Power:
He built and strengthened forts, establishing a strong defense system and a formidable navy.
Promotion of Marathi Language and Culture:
Shivaji Maharaj promoted the use of Marathi language and culture, fostering a sense of identity among his people.
Administrative Reforms:
He implemented efficient administrative systems, including revenue collection and justice administration.
Religious Tolerance:
Shivaji Maharaj respected all religions and ensured freedom of worship for his subjects.
